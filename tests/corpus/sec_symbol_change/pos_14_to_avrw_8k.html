Item 8.01 Other Events.

 

As previously announced, on October 14, 2022, Avenir Wellness Solutions, Inc. (the “Company”), a Delaware corporation formerly known as CURE Pharmaceutical Holding Corp., changed its name from “CURE Pharmaceutical Holding Corp.” to “Avenir Wellness Solutions, Inc.” On August 8, 2023, the Company was notified by the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority Inc. (“FINRA”) that it approved the Company’s ticker symbol change from “CURR” to “AVRW,” with an effective date of August 9, 2023, and that such ticker symbol change and effective date had been announced on FINRA’s Daily List on August 8, 2023. The Company’s CUSIP number remains 23127P104.

 

On August 8, 2023, the Company issued a press release announcing, among other things, its receipt of regulatory approval to change its ticker symbol to “AVRW,” such change to be effective at the stock market opening of August 9, 2023. A copy of such press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.
